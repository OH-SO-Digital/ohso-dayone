# Day-One Assignment — Product Image Syndication

Welcome. We sell delightful nonsense (Orphan Discobombulators, Emotional Support Anvils, and
the like) and we need their imagery wired up in our catalogue.

## What you have

In `data/`:
- **`products.csv`** — our product catalogue.
- **`images/`** — a folder of product images.
- **`mapping.json`** — the rules for which images go where.

## What we want

Produce an **import file** that attaches the right images to the right products, in the right
order, according to the mapping. Something we could hand to our catalogue system.

That's it. Ship what you'd be comfortable importing.

## Notes

- Work however you like; any language/tools.
- Timebox: ~90 minutes. We care more about how you reason than about a perfect result.
- If something about the data seems off, use your judgement — and tell us what you decided and why.

Good luck. Ask if you need anything.
